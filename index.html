About the Company

Your Reliable Partner
Since 2012

Ningbo Yod Import & Export Co., Ltd. is a Brand Holder established in 2012, headquartered in Zhejiang, China. We specialize in HVAC accessories, plumbing fittings, electrical supplies, and heating components — providing a one-stop sourcing solution for international buyers.

With an in-house team of mold designers, 10+ awarded patents, and a 5,000 sqm free warehousing facility, we deliver professional, flexible, and cost-efficient supply chain services to clients across North America, Europe, South America, and Oceania.
